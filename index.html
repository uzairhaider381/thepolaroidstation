Terms & Conditions

A gentle reminder to all our lovely customers!

  • Your event slot is NOT confirmed until the advance payment has been received.
  • Simply enquiring about a date or discussing availability does not mean the slot is reserved for you.
  • Slots are confirmed on a first-paid, first-confirmed basis.
  • If someone else confirms the same date and pays the advance before you, the slot will be allocated to them, even if you enquired earlier.
  • We cannot hold dates without a confirmed booking and advance payment.
  • Your advance payment is non-refundable.
  • We always try to use all the films included in your package.
  • If any films are left unused, the payment for them will not be refunded.
  • We always encourage you to manage and use all your Polaroid films during the event.
  • Your content creation data will be delivered within 24 hours of your event.
  • You have 15 days to request any re-edits for the photos, reel, or content we provide from your event.
